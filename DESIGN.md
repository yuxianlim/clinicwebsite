# Aven Clinic · Visual direction

Aven Clinic is a contemporary, English-first outpatient clinic: calm, precise, approachable and distinctive. The public website helps people understand care and book a visit; the workspace prioritizes legibility, operational status and clear next steps.

- Warm paper `#f8f7f3`, white surfaces and ink `#192738` form the base.
- Cobalt `#3158d8` identifies primary actions, links and selected navigation. Peach is a limited illustrative accent. Green, amber and red remain reserved for operational outcomes.
- Locally packaged Geist typography, generous section spacing, editorial headings and quiet borders establish hierarchy. Abstract forms and clinician monograms provide character without decorative botanical imagery or unrelated portrait photography.
- Public pages use a distinct primary booking action. The light staff workspace keeps administrative controls and status evidence compact, searchable and readable.
- Native modal dialogs, visible keyboard focus, comfortable control sizes and the existing six-viewport browser checks remain part of the design.

Visible copy is English-first. Stored practitioner names and identifiers remain unchanged; `practitionerLabel` provides English display labels for existing values. Future bilingual support should use a localization layer rather than changing stored data.
